# Business Goals

## Primary goals

1. **Absolute net return** — Produce positive absolute net returns after
   spread, commission, swap, slippage, and infrastructure costs.
2. **Capital efficiency** — Maximize return / max-drawdown ratio alongside
   absolute return.
3. **Autonomous operation** — Operate one XAUUSD account autonomously across
   separate intraday and swing strategy books.
4. **Decision lineage** — Preserve full lineage for every research, risk,
   portfolio, execution, and review decision.
5. **Validated promotion** — Advance strategies through research →
   paper → limited-live → scale validation gates, with out-of-sample
   evidence required at the research gate.
6. **Foundation for expansion** — Build a modular foundation for future
   multi-asset, multi-strategy, multi-broker, multi-account proprietary
   operations.

## Secondary goals

- Demonstrate stable unattended operation with safe degraded-state behavior.
- Produce institutional-grade reporting suitable for internal review and
  external audit.
- Generate reusable research artifacts (datasets, features, backtests,
  evaluations) that compound in value over time.
- Keep operating costs predictable relative to capital under management.

## Non-financial goals

- Auditability: every production decision traceable to inputs, code,
  prompt, model, and policy versions.
- Reproducibility: any historical decision can be replayed with identical
  results given the same inputs and versions.
- Interruption safety: any failure mode reaches a safe state rather than
  unbounded risk-taking.
- Governance integrity: human CIO authority over production admission,
  emergency stop, and restart is never bypassed by the system.
