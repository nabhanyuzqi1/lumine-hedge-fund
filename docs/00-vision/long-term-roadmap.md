# Long-Term Roadmap

The roadmap describes product evolution from the first XAUUSD pilot to the
internal hedge-fund OS destination. Each milestone is a product milestone,
not an implementation milestone. Implementation sequencing is produced in
Phase 14.

## Milestone 0 — Foundation (first release)

- Proprietary capital
- XAUUSD only
- One broker, one MT5 account
- Hybrid intraday / swing strategy books
- Autonomous trade operation within owner-set policy
- CIO kill switch and restart authority
- Research → paper → limited-live validation gates
- Sandbox for self-modifying candidates (no production promotion)
- Full decision lineage and audit trail

## Milestone 1 — Validated pilot

- Limited-live operation demonstrates acceptable risk-adjusted returns
- Risk-policy breach rate at zero
- Reconciliation drift at zero
- Unattended operation stable with safe degraded-state behavior
- CIO authorizes capital scaling

## Milestone 2 — Scaled XAUUSD

- Increased proprietary capital under management
- Additional validated strategy books within XAUUSD
- Improved regime detection and exposure adaptation
- Institutional reporting suitable for external audit

## Milestone 3 — Multi-instrument expansion

- Additional instruments (e.g., forex pairs, indices, commodities)
- Modular instrument abstraction validated by second asset class
- Cross-instrument risk aggregation and limits

## Milestone 4 — Multi-broker, multi-account

- Multiple brokers for redundancy and execution quality
- Multiple proprietary accounts with independent mandates
- Allocation logic across accounts
- Broker reconciliation at scale

## Milestone 5 — Research and adaptation maturity

- Sandbox self-modification produces CIO-approved production promotions
- Strategy / model / prompt lifecycle automation (with human gates)
- Long-horizon research memory and compounding knowledge base

## Milestone 6 — Internal hedge-fund OS

- Full proprietary fund operating system
- Multi-portfolio, multi-account, multi-asset autonomous operation
- Institutional-grade research, risk, execution, and review departments
- Optional future strategic pivots (managed fund, SaaS, marketplace) remain
  out of scope unless Phase 0 is revised

## Out of roadmap (unless strategy revised)

- External investor capital
- Retail signals or copy trading
- Client tenancy
- Strategy marketplace
- Institutional SaaS licensing
