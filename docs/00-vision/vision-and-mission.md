# Vision & Mission

## Vision

Build an internal AI-native hedge-fund operating system capable of
autonomously researching, deciding, executing, supervising, and learning
across proprietary portfolios — while every material decision remains
attributable, reproducible, observable, and interruptible.

## Mission

Prove repeatable absolute net returns from XAUUSD through governed
autonomous operation, beginning with one broker and one proprietary account,
then expand through validated strategy books, instruments, portfolios,
brokers, and accounts.

## Identity

Lumine is not a trading bot, expert advisor, retail signal provider, or
copy-trading product. It is an institutional-grade operating system for
proprietary capital, organized like a real hedge fund with research, risk,
portfolio, execution, and review functions — staffed by a combination of
deterministic software and LLM-based reasoning agents.

## Strategic destination

The long-term destination is an internal hedge-fund OS that operates the
owner's own portfolios and accounts. It is not a managed fund, institutional
SaaS, or strategy marketplace. Those destinations are documented as
non-goals unless the strategy is revised in a future Phase 0 update.
